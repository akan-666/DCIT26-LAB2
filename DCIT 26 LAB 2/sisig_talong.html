Crispy Sisig Talong

Ingredients

  • 4 pcs. eggplants (cubed)
  • Breading mix, flour, and paprika
  • Mayonnaise, minced garlic, lemon juice, and liquid seasoning

Procedure

  1. Coat eggplant cubes in batter and breading mixture.
  2. Fry until golden brown and crispy.
  3. Mix fried eggplant with onions and chilies.
  4. Toss with the prepared mayonnaise sauce and serve.
