Tortang Talong (Filipino Eggplant Omelet)

Ingredients

  • 4 small eggplants
  • 1 pound ground pork
  • 4 eggs
  • Oyster sauce, onions, and garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Bake eggplants for 40-60 mins until soft, then peel and flatten.
  2. Saute onions and garlic, then add ground pork and cook for 10 minutes. Stir in oyster sauce.
  3. Beat 4 eggs. Dip eggplant in egg, then fry in a pan.
  4. Spoon the pork mixture over the eggplant while cooking. Flip and finish.
